Why Arc

Why Arc fits a spending firewall. Four properties the design leans on.

A1

USDC is gas

Every budget, cap and rate is in dollars, gas included. There is no second, volatile token to reason about.

A2

Final in one block

One confirmation is final. "Spent" means spent, and revoking a mandate takes effect at once.

A3

Two fuses

The firewall can only sign against what's deposited in Gateway, so the worst case has a hard ceiling onchain. The firewall is the soft ceiling in front of it.

Firewall · soft
Gateway deposit · hard
A4

Pre-signing is the only checkpoint

Nanopayments settle later, in bulk, so nothing onchain warns you at the moment of a bad payment. The only place to stop one is before the signature.
